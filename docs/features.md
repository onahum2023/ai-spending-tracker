# Features and pages

What each page of the app does. Screenshots are in [screenshots.md](screenshots.md).

| Page | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Overview** (how much) | Three rows. **Row 1**: two cards of the same height. The hero card, content at its top left: a short label ("So far · through 12 Nov" for the month in progress, "So far this year · through 12 Nov" for the year in progress, otherwise "Total"; the period picker says which month or year), the period's total in a 48px figure and, for the month in progress, "at least €X by month end" with a hatched swatch and an Estimate pill. Beside it, one card of four stat tiles (equal heights, a vertical and a horizontal divider; each a label, a value and a meta line) that follow the period picker: *Month*: previous month, vs the same point in the previous month (a past month: vs the previous month), the year so far, average per month; *Year*: previous year, vs the same months of the previous year, average per month, highest month; *All time*: average per month, highest month, charges, providers. Only the comparison tile has an arrow. When either period holds one-time charges you confirmed, one short line gives the comparison without them. For the month in progress, when anything is still due, one clickable row under that line: an overlapping stack of dots in the colours of the providers still due (four at most, then "+N"), "N charges still due · €X" and a right-pointing chevron. It opens a popover anchored to the row (a bottom sheet on a phone), so the card keeps its height: every charge still due (avatar, provider, amount, expected date), the usage so far and **How is this estimated?**; Escape or a click outside closes it. Nothing is shown when nothing is still due, and a past month, a year and All time have no row. **Row 2**: the monthly chart (two thirds; stacked by provider, category or commitment type: subscription / usage / one-time, with a toggle top right) with the selected month solid and the others faded to about 38%, the month in progress topped by hatched segments for the charges still due and a "≥ €X" label, and **Who it went to** (one third): the top six providers for the period with avatar, amount, share and a bar, then the rest as Other (a grey "+N" avatar). **Click a bar to pick that month**, exactly as the date selector does (from Year view it switches to Month view for that month). **Row 3**: the period's charges, largest first (top 5, then **Show all**, with **Open in Transactions** to the same period pre-filtered), and the **Budget** card (one line and a button to set one, or a bar of recorded plus still due against it). Switch between Month / Year / All time at the top right; a Today button next to the arrows jumps to the current month or year (as far as the data reaches), and is disabled while it is already selected. On a phone the order is the hero card (with the still-due row), the tiles (two columns), the chart, Who it went to, the charges and the budget. |
| **Providers** (who) | All providers with all-time total, spending in the selected period, a **trend arrow** (the last 3 complete months against the 3 before, one-time charges left out; an arrow only when the change is more than 5% and more than €5, otherwise flat), share, transaction count, and first / most recent charge. Search, sort any column, and filter by category and commitment type. Click a provider for its detail page, laid out like the Overview: a chart card (that provider's months for the selected year, following the period picker; click a bar to pick that month; the charges still due stack on the current month, and the axis starts at the provider's first charge) beside an **At a glance** card (all-time total with rank and share, charges, typical charge, last charge, and the provider's settings: a tag for new charges and a category). Below, its **charge types** (the one place to say what each is): a row shows the label or statement description, what it is read as, charges, total, last charge and status pills ("App's guess" until you confirm it; price change, overlap, stopped); open a row to change its label or type and to see its schedule, next charge, price history, overlap and whether anything still charges. Charge types with the same label are one plan. Then its full transaction history. A "To review" column shows how many charge types you have not confirmed yet. |
| **Transactions** | Searchable, sortable table with original merchant descriptors and amounts. Filter by provider and date range (or "Use selected period"). Filters live in the URL, so provider pages and the overview link straight into a filtered view. Click a row to jump to its provider. Each charge can carry a tag (Personal, Business, a client), singly or for everything shown at once, and a charge imported by mistake can be deleted (with a confirmation). The type of a charge is shown but not changed here: it links to the provider page. |
| **Insights** | What the data says, in four groups. **Patterns**: monthly spending with a 3-month average, subscription, usage and one-time over time ("more tools or more usage?"), and recurring-looking patterns. **Shifts**: price changes ("went from €22.49 to €109.38 in Jun") and stopped subscriptions with a check that nothing still charges. **Suggestions**: overlapping tools ("2 chat assistants · €130/month combined") and what ending each would save. **Comparisons**: year-over-year by calendar month, yearly totals with like-for-like comparison, biggest increases and decreases by provider (last 3 months vs the 3 before, or year to date vs last year), and provider concentration. Anything the app worked out is marked **App's read**, with the reasoning behind a "How are these found?" toggle. See [Insights](how-it-works.md#insights). |
| **Data** | A row of tiles (transactions, total recorded, data through, where the data is stored), then import more CSVs (with a review step), export all transactions as CSV (budgets, corrections, labels, categories and tag rules are not in the file), export one quarter (optionally one tag) for expense claims, clear local data (requires typing `DELETE`), import history, and the duplicate policy in plain language. |

**The ledger is fact, everything else is inference.** Amounts, dates and providers are shown plainly. Anything the app worked out (a subscription, a next charge, a stopped one, an estimate, a charge's type) is marked as the app's read or an estimate, and the reasoning is one click away.

Works on desktop and mobile, in light and dark mode. The five main pages have no title of their own, with the period controls at the top right; a provider's page keeps its title. A top bar carries the five pages and **About** (the Welcome page); on a phone the five pages are a bottom tab bar. Every page is built from the same few components (card, stat tile, pill, provider avatar, table, button, input, segmented control) in `src/components/ui.tsx`, with the colours, type sizes and spacing in `src/tokens.css`. Provider colours are assigned once by all-time spend and stay the same across every chart; the 6 largest providers get their own colour (indigo, teal, orange, amber, pink, blue) and the rest share a grey "Other". Categories use the same hues (Domains is a deep pink), and the commitment types are Subscription indigo, Usage teal and One-time amber. The palette is validated for lightness, chroma, colour-blind separation and contrast in light and dark mode; the notes are in `src/lib/colors.ts`.
